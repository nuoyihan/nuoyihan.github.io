*We play a group of long-admired "high-art" pieces.<br>
But you might notice, we are exhibtied outdoors, instead of a white cube gallery.<br>
Don't be afraid. Come closer to us, and you will see some of our features are recycled paper and cardboard,<br>
you will see we are not perfectly still -though we try our best to be, we have to breathe and occasionally tremble.<br>
Maybe you will start to take pictures of us or with us,<br>
As we stand here for hours being shown, we can't help to think about the long hours our creators devoted into creating us...<br>
Wait, did you spot the cameras!?<br>
Each of us carries a hidden camera somewhere, we are the silent observers.<br>
What is the theme of this exhibition, you may ask.<br>
There may be a theme, there may not be a theme, or there may be several themes,<br>
-for we are just artworks in an exhibition.*
